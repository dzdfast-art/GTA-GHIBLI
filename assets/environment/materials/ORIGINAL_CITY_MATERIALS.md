# GTA GHIBLI — Original City Material Set

Eight original, procedurally authored 2048×2048 SVG surface materials created for this project. They are connected to the open-world district renderer: roads, brick and concrete facades, corrugated metal, tiled roofs, sidewalk paving, storefront glazing, and garage shutters. The vector source contains individually varied chips, grain, weathering, seams, scratches, reflections and cracks. No third-party game assets or texture packs are included in this material set.
