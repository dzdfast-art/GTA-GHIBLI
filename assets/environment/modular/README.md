# Modular environment collection

Original low-poly OBJ collections for GTA GHIBLI. The OBJ files are authored as reusable grouped mesh parts and share `modular_environment.mtl`.

- `village_house_collection.obj`: 16 varied village house shells, pitched roofs, doors, windows, frames and stone details.
- `harbor_warehouse_collection.obj`: industrial warehouse shells, roof forms, loading doors, awnings and vents.
- `road_street_collection.obj`: road tiles, dashed lane markings, sidewalks, curbs and drains.
- `coastal_pier_collection.obj`: pier decks, plank surfaces, support posts and bollards.
- `market_block_collection.obj`: market structures, awnings, counters, shelves and crates.

Import as OBJ in Godot 4, verify material assignment, then split/place the grouped objects in scenes as needed. These are asset collections, not yet placed in the playable world. Geometry uses simple low-poly box and roof primitives to keep mobile rendering economical. No third-party game assets are included.
